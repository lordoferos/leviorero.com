I started this blog to share my thoughts with the world. 
We often get lost in survival mode and lose focus on what really matters. I am particularly wary of our devices and social media and the time we spend there. For lack of a better word, it is escapism. Trying to numb yourself after an overstimulating and tiring day at work. The problem is these social media apps are still overstimulating and constantly messing with our sleep. Rest has now become an active job that has immense benefits. Keep it that way and always protect it.

I am keeping this blog to remind myself of a couple of things to stay sane in this supercharged world.
When was the last time you called those close to you even before you needed anything from them? Your parents. Your children. Your friends. Check when you call them next. Is it because you needed something or just a simple hello? It’s me. I am here. I missed you and wanted to talk to you. Something like that.
That should work.

We were designed to be on the move. Every hour and every day unless we are asleep. Modern day life has made this into an option you can take (or not).
Take that choice to move. Dance and walk around. Go to the gym whenever you can. Move that body and make sure you keep your flexibility. If you don’t use it, you will lose it.

We have limited time in this world. Yet while trying to be diplomatic, we end up saying yes to things we hate or do not care about. I will remind you to say no faster and mean it. Saves everyone their time. People will be disappointed or angry but that is on them. As long as your conscience is clear, say no when you want to.

If life gives you lemons, make lemonade. We were born into a financial system that now promotes consumerism. Getting the latest gadget, the latest holiday, the latest car. All while trying to please.  A financial law that will always stand the test of time is spending less than you earn. Downgrade and cut down expenses when you have to. Accept your current situation and work towards a better future and income. But work within your means. This reminds me of a great book called “The Richest Man in Babylon” by George S. Clason. Give it time and watch compounding do its magic. You will be less stressed for it.

On social media, stats show that you have thousands of followers or friends. They may know of you but they are not your friends. Keep a few friends who will tell you the truth. If the truth makes you walk away, then you are not a real friend. The reverse is true. Tell those you consider your friends the truth. If they walk away, then they were not real friends in the first place.

How many books or articles have you read this year? Were they related to your work? We are in the era where access is unlimited. Read about space, read about World War I, read about native tree species of the Amazon etc. Read anything that looks interesting. Now and then, you will bump into gold.

You will learn why it is important to apologise quickly without a “but”. Move on and your mind will thank you for it. You will feel lighter and not waste mental resources thinking about those who wronged you.
While at it, do not forget to take real rest. Real rest is not sitting on a couch and watching TV or browsing TikTok. Rest by sleeping and stay away from that laptop and phone. Do it a few times a year and journal your thoughts while at it. Disconnect and the great ideas and solutions will come. Sometimes, all you needed was silence.

Lastly, what is “enough” for you? Will you keep chasing and chasing until the last day? When will you start enjoying life? How do you define enjoying life? Maybe start with that. Then work towards that and remember to stay contented. Key to happiness in my opinion. Until the next one, goodbye :)
